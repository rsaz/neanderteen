Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy covers Neanderteen Publisher, the internal tool the Neanderteen show uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the show's creator signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • One Google permission: youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos") for the Neanderteen channel, used only to upload the show's finished episodes and Shorts, with their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedule.
  • The tool does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other Google account's data.

How credentials are kept

The OAuth refresh token for the channel is stored only as an encrypted secret in the show's private GitHub repository (GitHub Actions secrets) and on the creator's own computer. It is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Neanderteen Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach the show through the Neanderteen YouTube channel.
