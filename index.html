Neanderteen

Three cave teens. Frozen 40,000 years ago. Thawed by hot chocolate. Enrolled in high school by mistake.

Neanderteen is an original animated comedy series about Oog, Nika and Bonk, three teenagers from the Ice Age trying to survive modern life without anyone finding out they're cavemen. Phones, pizza, Wi-Fi, the school bus, and a museum curator who wants his exhibit back... nothing is safe.

Neanderteen Publisher

Neanderteen Publisher is the show's own internal tool. After an episode is approved, it uploads the finished videos to the Neanderteen YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the show's creator, for that one channel. See the privacy policy and terms.
